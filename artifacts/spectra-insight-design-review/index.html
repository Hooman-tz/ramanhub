Recommendation

Choose the signal,
not the noise.

All three directions hold the same product boundary. They differ in how much warmth, density, and visual memory they bring to the work.

01

Helix

Best default for the core product. The clearest hierarchy between private work and published evidence, with enough warmth for daily use.

Recommend for: product foundation
02

Field Notes

Best for onboarding and researcher identity. Editorial typography gives the profile and publication story more human presence.

Recommend for: profile + storytelling
03

Signal Atlas

Best for discovery and large-scale browsing. A brighter instrument-panel language makes provenance and navigation highly scannable.

Recommend for: Commons + exploration

Notes for review

Readable under pressure.

  • Charts use a solid trace, point markers, grid, axis labels, and a text summary — never color alone.
  • Private, public, and social states use labels, icons, and structure in addition to color.
  • All controls are keyboard reachable; focus states and 44px mobile targets are part of the treatment.
  • Unavailable analytics are explicitly labeled as future metric slots, not live claims.
